MISSION 4 OF 5 ⚙️ Axis Customization — Order the Steps

You need to customize the vertical (Y) axis of your chart in Google Sheets to start at 5,000 instead of 0. Drag the steps into the correct order to complete this workflow.

  • ⠿ ? In the Chart Editor panel, click the "Customize" tab
  • ⠿ ? Set the "Min" value to 5000 in the axis range fields
  • ⠿ ? Click on the chart to select it (a blue border appears)
  • ⠿ ? Scroll down and click "Vertical axis" to expand that section
  • ⠿ ? Click the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right of the chart → select "Edit chart"
