📚 Key Takeaways

  • Principal = original loan amount (not including interest)
  • Monthly Rate = APR ÷ 12 (convert annual to monthly)
  • PMT formula = =PMT(rate, nper, pv)
  • Early payments = more interest; later payments = more principal
  • Data validation protects model integrity from bad inputs
