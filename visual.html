The 5/50 Rule: Mathematical Constraints
Domain 4.2 · Regulatory Constraints
Briefing: The 5/50 Rule is a mathematical regulatory constraint used in financial modeling. It states: No single budget category should exceed 50% of total funds, and no single investment should be less than 5% of total funds — ensuring balance and meaningful allocation.
📏 The 5/50 Rule
Maximum per category: 50% of total windfall
Minimum meaningful allocation: 5% of total windfall
Windfall amount: $50,000
You're reviewing three proposed budgets. Which one PASSES the 5/50 Rule?

Domain 4.2 · Financial Functions
Briefing: You're financing a $12,000 car loan at 6% annual interest over 3 years. You must use Google Sheets' PMT function to calculate the fixed monthly payment. Remember: rate must be monthly (÷12), nper must be months (×12), and pv must be negative.
📋 PMT Syntax
=PMT(rate, nper, pv)
rate = monthly rate | nper = total months | pv = -principal
