📚 Key Takeaways — Form 1099-K
🏦

Payment Settlement Entities (PSEs) like PayPal, Venmo, and Stripe send Form 1099-K to report gross digital payments.

💰

Gross vs. Net: The 1099-K shows your GROSS income. You still subtract business expenses to find your taxable net income.

📅

Monthly breakdowns help you track income patterns and verify the total matches your own records.

⚠️

Threshold: For 2024, PSEs must file a 1099-K if you received over $5,000 in payments (threshold lowering to $600 in future years).
